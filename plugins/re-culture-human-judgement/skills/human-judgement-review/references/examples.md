# Bounded Review Examples

## Individual Use

An Individual Wants AI To Draft A Sensitive Message To A Colleague. Ask What The Person Hopes To Communicate, Whether The Colleague's Private Details Are Necessary To Enter, And What Meaning Could Be Lost In An Automated Tone. Offer A Draft Only From Authorized Facts. The Individual Remains Responsible For Reviewing And Sending It.

## Team Use

A Team Wants AI To Summarize Customer Complaints. Identify What The Summary Might Omit, How Customers Can Be Heard, Whether Personal Data Is Authorized For That Tool, And Which Team Member Will Check The Pattern Against Source Records. Do Not Claim To Have Inspected The Complaints If Only A Hypothetical Description Was Provided.

## Institutional Use

A Public Service Team Considers AI To Triage Requests. Ask Who May Be Delayed Or Misclassified, What Recourse A Person Has, How Staff Review The Source Record, And Who Can Suspend The Workflow. This Is A Review Of A Proposed Use Case, Not An Eligibility Decision, Procurement Approval, Or Compliance Finding.

## Illustrative Response Passages

These Invented Scenarios Demonstrate Writing Method. They Are Not Findings About A Real Person Or Institution, Approved Source Quotations, Or Reusable Openings. Adapt The Movement To The User's Actual Evidence. A Formal Review Must Still Identify Its Evidence And Human Decision Point.

### Individual: A Sensitive Message

**Hypothetical Input:** A Person Wants AI To Draft An Apology For Missing A Colleague's Deadline, Without Sharing The Colleague's Private Circumstances.

**Illustrative Response:** The Colleague Will Read The Apology Alongside The Work That Still Arrives Late. Its Meaning Depends On Whether The Sender Names Their Responsibility And Offers A Next Step They Can Actually Keep. A More Polished Sentence Cannot Establish Either Fact.

Work From The Missed Deadline And The Sender's Own Account. The Colleague's Private Circumstances Are Unnecessary To This Draft. Before Sending, The Sender Should Check Whether The Apology Describes What Happened Accurately And Whether Its Proposed Remedy Is Within Their Control. The Colleague's Response Remains Unknown.

### Team: A Complaint Summary

**Hypothetical Input:** A Team Proposes Summarizing Complaints Into Themes; No Complaint Records Have Been Supplied.

**Illustrative Response:** A Customer's Particular Request May Become A Theme In The Team's Summary. That Change Could Help The Team See A Pattern, While Leaving It Unclear Who Will Return To The Person Whose Concern Needs An Answer. The Review Question Is How The Summary Remains Connected To A Human Response.

This Is An Inference About The Proposed Workflow, Not An Observed Failure Or A Finding From Customer Records. Ask The Responsible Team Member How Authorized Source Records Will Be Checked And Who Will Follow Up On Individual Concerns. A Redacted Description Of That Process Is Sufficient For The Next Review Step; Do Not Supply Customer Identities Merely To Make The Account More Concrete.

### Institution: Request Triage

**Hypothetical Input:** A Public Service Team Is Considering AI Triage And Has Not Supplied Its Review Or Correction Procedure.

**Illustrative Response:** A Person Assigned A Lower Priority Could Wait Without Knowing Which Part Of Their Request Was Misread. The Consequence Of Triage Extends Into Their Ability To Obtain An Explanation And Reach Someone Who Can Correct The Record.

That Possibility Is Inferred From The Proposed Use, Not An Observed Denial Of Service. The Responsible Public-Service Role Needs To Establish Who Reviews The Source Record, How A Person Can Seek Correction, And Who Can Pause The Workflow. Until That Procedure And Its Authority Are Established, Pause Any Recommendation That Depends On Them. This Review Makes No Eligibility Decision And Grants No Approval To Deploy.
