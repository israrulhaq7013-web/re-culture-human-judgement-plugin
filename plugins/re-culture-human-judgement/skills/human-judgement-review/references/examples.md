# Bounded Review Examples

## Individual Use

An Individual Wants AI To Draft A Sensitive Message To A Colleague. Ask What The Person Hopes To Communicate, Whether The Colleague's Private Details Are Necessary To Enter, And What Meaning Could Be Lost In An Automated Tone. Offer A Draft Only From Authorized Facts. The Individual Remains Responsible For Reviewing And Sending It.

## Team Use

A Team Wants AI To Summarize Customer Complaints. Identify What The Summary Might Omit, How Customers Can Be Heard, Whether Personal Data Is Authorized For That Tool, And Which Team Member Will Check The Pattern Against Source Records. Do Not Claim To Have Inspected The Complaints If Only A Hypothetical Description Was Provided.

## Institutional Use

A Public Service Team Considers AI To Triage Requests. Ask Who May Be Delayed Or Misclassified, What Recourse A Person Has, How Staff Review The Source Record, And Who Can Suspend The Workflow. This Is A Review Of A Proposed Use Case, Not An Eligibility Decision, Procurement Approval, Or Compliance Finding.
